## Open the history collection

Open Secondary, then choose **History studio**. Select **Philippine History** or **World History**, choose Secondary 1–4, or search for a period or topic. The two tracks also appear in the normal World lessons subject selector.

The gateway starts in Trial. This update retains the original Primary and Secondary access codes. **No additional history code is needed.** Secondary Full opens all history lessons. Primary access and content are unchanged.

## Activity sequence

**Read:** Start with the historical overview and learning goal. Review the references. Open the milestone study guide before sequencing.

**Timeline Builder:** Rearrange event cards using the up/down buttons. These controls work with mouse, touch or keyboard. Optional date hints are available and recorded. The result reports completion, number of checks and use of hints, not an exam accuracy percentage. Some sequences include comparison anchors outside the lesson’s main period, which are labelled as such. Date ranges are placed by their beginnings; approximate dates remain marked. Chronology does not by itself demonstrate causation.

**Source Detective:** Read the labelled source brief and, where possible, open the original reference. Choose whether the response is based on the brief only or also on the original. Write or dictate discussion notes for origin/context, purpose/audience, evidence/inference, and limits/corroboration. Teacher guidance is available. The teacher can mark four criteria from 0–2, for a total of 8, or save an ungraded reflection. The software does not assess essays automatically.

**Practice:** Use the compatible quiz, controlled-answer, matching, study-card, discussion and other existing formats. Sensitive history restricts competitive teams, AR, matching and the proposed-answer Claim Checker. Attempting to launch an excluded mode falls back to the ordinary quiz. No graphic material, atrocity simulations or victim–perpetrator role-play is included.

## Teacher editing, queues and results

History uses the existing Secondary lesson queue and results, not a disconnected app. In Full, open a lesson and choose **Edit a copy**. The editor preserves its period, location, chronological sequence, source-brief metadata and sensitive-topic setting. The timeline and source brief use labelled JSON fields. Source references use IDs from the included catalogue. Custom historical assertions and edited dates must be checked by the teacher.

Printing a Full lesson includes its overview, references, a milestone study guide, practice questions and a separate answer-key section. Browser results/CSV distinguish source-analysis rubric ratings, ungraded reflection, chronology checks and quiz accuracy. The gateway homepage and existing teacher customisation remain available.

## Sources and historical care

Historical overviews are sourced historical accounts, not fictional city projects. The earlier global scenario banks retain their fictional-data labels. Source briefs are newly written paraphrases, **not facsimiles or invented eyewitness quotations**. The app differentiates a historical object/text from a later museum, archive or institutional interpretation. External references need Internet access and should be reviewed by the teacher before use. Some lead to scholarly documents or sensitive collections.

The collection draws on the National Museum of the Philippines, NHCP materials, museum collections, archives, historical documents, the United Nations and other named references. Each lesson links the sources used for its account and milestone comparisons. Primary documents, later interpretations and uncertainty should be discussed separately. Interpretation does not mean giving unsupported denial equal status with established evidence.

## Updating your published site

Extract **Wonder-Global-Studio-History-GitHub.zip** and replace the existing published **index.html** with the supplied file. Keep the same repository/site address and directory to preserve the browser storage namespace. Refresh after publication. The unchanged stored code is revalidated against the new Full package. A changed browser profile, cleared site storage or different URL may require code entry again.

The public ZIP contains only the protected gateway, documentation and optional local launchers. Both Full editions remain separately encrypted. **Do not upload the private Owner Kit, private codes, unlocked Full exports or the inputs folder.** The code scheme remains shared-code, local-browser access, not account-based or tamper-proof licensing.

The app was not deployed to your GitHub repository in this update. Test the published version with a classroom browser, including saved-code restoration. Ordinary activities do not need a webcam. Optional existing webcam tracking still needs suitable browser support, permission and its model download.

Secondary → History studio → Philippine History → Secondary 2 → **Reform in print**. Read the overview; use Timeline Builder; then switch to Source Detective and discuss why a novel and a later historian’s explanation are different kinds of evidence.
